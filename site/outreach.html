The Outreach Center

Tool 12 · The Outreach Center · Money · current

Stage 4 · Growth · Pricing: Free · Added 2026-10-12 · Updated 2026-10-12

Your saved shelf

The outbound side of the Deal Room: build a brand pipeline, write pitches that respect inboxes, quote from the 2026 rate card, and read a contract before you sign it. Everything lives in your browser, no accounts, no uploads, no data leaves the machine.

Got an inbound offer to benchmark instead? The Deal Room manages the whole deal from offer to paid. The outreach rulebook lives in the Phase 5 chapter.

1 · Outreach pipeline

Your CRM-lite for outbound: who you pitched, who replied, when the one nudge goes out. Follow up exactly once, then move on. ~5% reply rate is normal; the system works on volume.

    Pipeline lives in this browser only (localStorage). A "no" is data, mark it passed and keep pitching the next brand on the list.

    2 · Pitch email generator

    Three templates from the sponsorship outreach playbook: cold pitch, the one 7-day nudge, and the warm intro. Fill the fields, every [bracket] is a placeholder YOU complete with your real numbers. The generator never invents a stat for you.

    Anti-spam contract: one targeted email per brand, never bulk blasts. No reply to the cold pitch gets exactly one nudge, a second nudge is spam. Never pitch a brand whose product you haven't vetted; your audience blames you, not the brand.

    3 · What should I charge?

    The 2026 rate card, interactive: your tier range, a CPM cross-check against your expected views, and the negotiation guidance that travels with every number. Ranges, never quotes, never promises.

    Tier ranges from the 2026 rate card (benchmarks disagree 2–3× within tiers, niche, engagement, and geography move the real number). Sponsors buy views, not subs: quote off your last-10-video average. At equal followers, YouTube pays ~1.7× Instagram, TikTok ~0.85×, re-verified against October 2026 agency tables.

    4 · Contract red-flag checklist

    Read the paper before you sign it. Check every clause the contract actually contains, exclusivity, usage rights, payment terms, kill fees, the traps hiding in the boilerplate. Your selections persist in this browser.

    This checklist scores the contract's structure, the offer's pay is benchmarked in §3, and the Deal Room's checklist covers offer terms. Not legal advice: a negotiation aid only. Contracts above $500 deserve a real lawyer's eyes.
