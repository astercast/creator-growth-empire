Your saved shelf

Your rate card, built in the open: pick your platform, your follower band, and the format you sell most. Every row prices itself from a named benchmark in this site's data and prints its reasoning next to the number — and a row with no benchmark behind it says so, in words, instead of borrowing one.

Pricing one live deal instead? The Deal Builder stacks a multi-deliverable quote. Brand already replied? The Negotiation Kit builds the counter. This page is the card you publish before either of those conversations start.

Build Your card Export

1 · Build the card

One primary action: your platform, your band, your format. Usage rights and whitelisting ride on the primary format as separate priced lines, the way the benchmarks say they should be quoted.

2 · Your rate card

Every row carries its basis: benchmark (the 2026 tier table or CPM band), reported (what creators actually quote), single-source (one report, flagged), proxy (a labeled reference where this platform has no benchmark of its own), or no data — the honest blank. Under the card: the newest Algorithm Watch changes for your platform, because a rate quoted against a rule that just moved is a rate quoted blind.

3 · Export — the card as plaintext

The whole card, reasoning included, ready to paste into a media kit, an email, or your notes. The reasoning travels with the numbers on purpose: a rate you can explain is a rate you can hold.

Ranges, not quotes. Benchmarks disagree 2–3× within tiers in the source data; niche, engagement, format, and usage move the real number as much as follower count does. Planning aid — not financial, legal, or tax advice.
