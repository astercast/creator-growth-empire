Your saved shelf

The Deal Pipeline totals what brands owe you into one number, and the Follow-Through Queue only surfaces an invoice once it is old enough to chase. This page registers all of it: every invoiced deal on the board, fresh ones included, with the amount, the date it entered the invoiced stage, how long it has been outstanding, and its late-payment script ready to open, plus one copyable register for the whole record.

Deals are entered on the Deal Pipeline, this register reads the same board (same browser, same localStorage). The money that has landed lives in the Sponsor Income Ledger. This page adds no pricing, no wording, and no thresholds of its own; the day's sends stay with the Follow-Through Queue.

The register The export

1 · The register: invoiced deals, longest outstanding first

A deal is registered from the day it enters the invoiced stage, completeness is the register's job, so no follow-up threshold decides what appears here. The invoiced date is the stage entry date, the only invoice timing the board carries. Demo invoiced deals are counted and excluded, never registered as real money owed.

2 · The register as plaintext

A planning aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Amounts are the figures you entered on the board; the total owed is the Deal Pipeline's invoiced outstanding figure, listed, never re-derived from any rate card.
