The five structures

From the Short-Form Playbook chapter — craft formulas for the first 1–3 seconds, not platform documentation. Each card shows the structure, a worked example, and your topic dropped into the blank.

Hook Library 2.0 — six patterns from October 2026

Distilled from the October 2026 TikTok trend reports (SocialPilot, New Engen, Later, MediaNug, Virlo). Trends expire in weeks; the containers below — reveal, confession, contrast — keep working. Steal the structure, never the sound. Each card names the mechanic it exploits, so you pick patterns for the signal you actually need.

1. The Costume Reveal Walk

Formula: open on a striking walk toward the camera — low angle, full commitment — then the zoom-out reveals the transformation.
Exploits: completion + watch time (the promised visual reveal is an open loop).
Spotted in: Ramalama Costume Walk — 430.7K videos (SocialPilot, Sept 25–Oct 5, 2026).
Adapt: the walk is a container — swap the costume for your reveal: the finished product, the before/after, the result of the experiment.

2. The Leaving Edit

Formula: "I left [X] behind" — a confession montage of walking away from a habit, job, or belief, cut to a wistful track.
Exploits: sends + shares (an identity confession people DM to friends — "this is so you").
Spotted in: Patient Zero edits — wedding edits and "leaving something behind" audio, 149.3K videos (SocialPilot; Virlo, Oct 2026).
Adapt: any transformation arc in your niche — the diet you quit, the tool you dropped, the belief you outgrew.

3. The Joy Contrast

Formula: upbeat, happy-dance energy layered over chaos — the track and the caption contradict each other.
Exploits: rewatches + comments (the contradiction makes viewers watch twice and comment "same").
Spotted in: Be OK / "I just want to be OK" — happy-dance-over-chaos (New Engen; Later, Oct 2, 2026).
Adapt: pair your realest caption with your most upbeat 3 seconds. The clash is the hook.

4. The Hidden Confession

Formula: "Text from -A" framing: "POV: someone just exposed the thing I've been hiding about [X]."
Exploits: watch time + comments (social tension creates a stay-to-the-end promise; the comments become the confessional).
Spotted in: Text From "-A" — 53.5K videos (MediaNug, 10/1/26; Later, Oct 2, 2026).
Adapt: confess a niche truth your audience thinks they're alone in. Specificity beats drama.

5. The Cozy Bed

Formula: romanticize a routine — slow shots of the ordinary process, a jazz/piano bed underneath, text overlay naming the ordinary thing as the good thing.
Exploits: saves + search (save-for-later comfort content; keyword-rich captions ride TikTok search).
Spotted in: cozy-fall mood beds — 5 of Metricool's Oct 1, 2026 top-10 sounds are jazz/piano/ambient (Virlo).
Adapt: any "day in my process" content becomes a save magnet with the right bed and a keyword-forward caption.

6. The Upside-Down Flip

Formula: shoot the "before" upside down or mid-gesture; flip to the "after" on the beat — the transition IS the hook.
Exploits: completion + shares (the visual trick delays the payoff past the swipe point).
Spotted in: "Upside down" before/after main-character transitions (Later, Oct 2, 2026).
Adapt: any transformation — edits, builds, makeovers, data reveals. Film the flip, not the filter.
