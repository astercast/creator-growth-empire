Read Phase 5 twice

If you only read one phase, read Phase 5, twice. Everyone skips to the money chapter and wonders why the math doesn't work. It doesn't work because phases 1 through 4 are the product. The stack is just the cash register on top of an audience that trusts you. I put monetization fifth on purpose.
