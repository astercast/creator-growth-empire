How this tool thinks

Two rule sets, both sourced and labeled with their observation dates. Current (through Jan 31, 2027): full YPP = 1,000 subs + 4,000 qualified watch hours/12mo or 10M qualified Shorts views/90d; fan-funding tier = 500 subs + 3 uploads/90d + 3,000 hours/12mo or 3M Shorts views/90d (YouTube published eligibility, restated by AIR Media-Tech and multi-source press). 2027 (from Feb 1, 2027): new applicants = 1,000 subs + 8,000 hours/365d or 20M Shorts views/90d; the Shorts Creator Pool + Shorts Premium floor = 10M qualified Shorts views/90d, checked monthly; a new partner activity test (1,000 hours/365d, 1M Shorts views/90d, or 2 longs / 5 Shorts per 90 days); fan-funding unchanged.
