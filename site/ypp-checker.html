How this tool thinks

Two rule sets, both sourced and labeled with their observation dates. Current (through Jan 31, 2027): full YPP = 1,000 subs + 4,000 qualified watch hours/12mo or 10M qualified Shorts views/90d; fan-funding tier = 500 subs + 3 uploads/90d + 3,000 hours/12mo or 3M Shorts views/90d (YouTube published eligibility, restated by AIR Media-Tech and multi-source press). 2027 (from Feb 1, 2027): new applicants = 1,000 subs + 8,000 hours/365d or 20M Shorts views/90d; the Shorts Creator Pool + Shorts Premium floor = 10M qualified Shorts views/90d, checked monthly; a new partner activity test (1,000 hours/365d, 1M Shorts views/90d, or 2 longs / 5 Shorts per 90 days); fan-funding unchanged. Announced by YouTube Aug 10, 2026; reported consistently by AIR Media-Tech, AuditSocials, Tablet News, GSMDome, and others. The Oct-1-2026 Shorts originality update (announced by Creator Liaison Rene Ritchie via Creator Insider) is surfaced as a warning whenever your content mix risks it. Thresholds are program rules, not forecasts, they change. Verify live in YouTube Studio's Earn tab. An educational aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice.
