Deal Builder

Deal Builder

Used in Stage 5, Monetization & Career. Free.

Your saved shelf

The package layer: turn deliverables into a line-item quote from the 2026 rate card, a ready-to-send proposal built from that quote, and a deal checklist built from the actual terms. Everything runs in your browser, no accounts, no uploads, no data leaves the machine.

Got an inbound offer to benchmark instead? The Deal Analyzer evaluates the brand's terms. Running outbound? The Outreach Center owns the pipeline and pitch templates. The deep layer: The 2026 Sponsor Playbook.

1 · Your package

Add each deliverable as its own line, platform, format, how many. The quote prices every line from the 2026 rate card, then stacks usage, whitelisting, and exclusivity as separate line items.

2 · The quote

Your line-item quote lands here, base fees, usage, whitelisting, exclusivity, the top-of-range ask, and the ~23% expected landing zone.

3 · Exclusivity & term checks

Window mismatches, broad-lock warnings, and the overlaps only you can verify.

4 · The proposal

Built from your actual quote, every stat you haven't supplied stays a [bracket] for you to fill. The generator never invents a number.

5 · The deal checklist

Generated from the terms you just built, usage, payment, revisions, exclusivity, FTC disclosure, deliverables, your walk-away line. Tick them off as the contract answers each one. Your checks persist in this browser.

Negotiation aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Rates are reported 2026 benchmarks (ranges, never quotes). The checklist is not legal advice; contracts above $500 deserve a real lawyer's eyes.
