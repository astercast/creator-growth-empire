Your saved shelf

The Deal Pipeline totals everything a brand paid into one number. This page ledgers it: each paid deal under the month it entered the paid stage, a running cumulative, how much of the collected total rides on each brand, and which platform the money came through, plus one copyable ledger for the whole record.

Deals are entered on the Deal Pipeline, this ledger reads the same board (same browser, same localStorage). The paid deals as a re-approach list live in the Repeat Sponsor Book. This page adds no pricing of its own; its only arithmetic is summing and grouping the amounts you entered.

The ledger The export

1 · The ledger: paid deals by month, brand, and platform

A deal is ledgered under the month it entered the paid stage, the only payment timing the board carries. That is a stage-entry month, not a clearing or payout date, and the ledger claims none. A paid deal whose stage entry date cannot be read is ledgered under Undated, flagged, and still counted, never dropped and never guessed into a month. Demo paid deals are counted and excluded, never ledgered as real income.

2 · The ledger as plaintext

A planning aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Amounts are the figures you entered on the board; the collected total is the Deal Pipeline's paid total, grouped, never re-derived from any rate card.
