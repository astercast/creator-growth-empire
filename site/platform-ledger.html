Your saved shelf

The money tools total the board by deal, by month, by invoice, and by brand, but never by platform across every stage at once: the income ledger splits only the collected money by platform, and the brand ledger lists a brand's platforms without totalling them. This page ledgers the whole board by platform: every platform with its total across all stages, how much of it has been collected, how much is owed, how much is still open, and its share of the whole pipeline, plus one copyable ledger for the whole record.

Deals are entered on the Deal Pipeline, this ledger reads the same board (same browser, same localStorage). Each brand as one relationship total lives in the Brand Ledger; the collected money by month in the Sponsor Income Ledger. This page adds no pricing, no wording, and no thresholds of its own; its only arithmetic is summing and grouping the amounts you entered, by platform and stage.

The ledger The export

1 · The ledger: platforms, largest total first

Collected is the paid stage, owed is the invoiced stage, and still open is lead, negotiating, terms set, and live. A platform total is a sum of current-stage amounts, the board carries no record of stages a deal has already left, so this is not a platform history, and no stage-entry date is presented as anything else. Demo deals are counted and excluded, never ledgered as real platform business.

2 · The ledger as plaintext

A planning aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Amounts are the figures you entered on the board; the pipeline total is the Deal Pipeline's pipeline value, grouped by platform, never re-derived from any rate card.
