DEAL ROOM 2.0 Rate card v1.5 — tier ranges, real quoted prices, and the Algorithm Watch digest in one room.

The full deal pipeline: log offers, score them against the 2026 rate card, price usage rights as separate line items, rehearse what to say, and export a clean memo. Everything lives in your browser — no accounts, no uploads.

One-off offer to benchmark first? Start with the Sponsor Deal Analyzer, then log it here to manage the whole deal. Pitching brands outbound instead? The Outreach Center runs the pipeline, the pitch emails, and the contract read. Mid-negotiation and need the counter, the exact reply, and your walk-away number? The Negotiation Kit builds all three from this room's data — and reads your tracker totals for its walk-away panel.

Tracker Pipeline Usage pricing 2026 rate card Scripts Red flags Algorithm Watch Memo

1 · Deal tracker

    Deals live in this browser only (localStorage). Score each deal against the 2026 rate card — fair / under / over — with the ~23%-above-accepted honesty note: creators list rates ~23% above what they accept, so quote high and expect to be negotiated down. Each card also checks the offer against what creators typically quote for the format (Cheerful, Sep 2026 — reported from real negotiations).

    2 · Pipeline summary

    Your numbers only — sums of the offers you logged, grouped by stage. No close-probability weighting: a signed deal is money on paper, an offer is a conversation, and this panel never confuses the two.

    3 · Usage-rights pricing calculator

    Multipliers are reported 2026 research ranges — the wave-2 band puts paid-ad usage at +50–100% on top of the post fee. Whitelisting and buyouts have no safe research multiplier, so the calculator refuses to invent one.

    4 · The 2026 rate card, in full

    The same data the tracker scores against — every figure with its basis. Reported means measured or compiled from real 2026 rate data; modeled means a source's own planning model, shown separately and never blended into the reported ranges. Full prose edition: The 2026 Rate Card.

    5 · Negotiation scripts

    Fill in your context once — every script below updates live, and each copies to your clipboard with one tap.

    6 · Red-flag checklist

    Scores the deal's contract structure — not its pay, which the tracker benchmarks separately. Check everything the offer actually contains; your selections persist in this browser.

    7 · Algorithm Watch digest

    The platform changes that move deal prices — policy, monetization, and ranking shifts from the last 90 days, each dated, sourced, and confidence-flagged, with a plain read on what it means for your next negotiation. The full feed lives at Algorithm Watch.

    8 · Copy my deal memo

    A one-page summary for your records: terms, rate-card score, red flags, and your counters. A negotiation aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Benchmarks disagree 2–3x within tiers — verify against current creator rate reports before you quote.
