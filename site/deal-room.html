The Deal Room

Manage the whole deal: track offers through signing, price usage rights, rehearse negotiations, export your memo.

TIER 2 // MONEY

The full deal pipeline: log offers, score them against the 2026 rate card, price usage rights as separate line items, rehearse what to say, and export a clean memo. Everything lives in your browser — no accounts, no uploads.

One-off offer to benchmark first? Start with the Sponsor Deal Analyzer, then log it here to manage the whole deal. Pitching brands outbound instead? The Outreach Center runs the pipeline, the pitch emails, and the contract read.

1 · Deal tracker

    Deals live in this browser only (localStorage). Score each deal against the 2026 rate card — fair / under / over — with the ~23%-above-accepted honesty note: creators list rates ~23% above what they accept, so quote high and expect to be negotiated down.

    2 · Usage-rights pricing calculator

    Multipliers are reported 2026 research ranges — the wave-2 band puts paid-ad usage at +50–100% on top of the post fee. Whitelisting and buyouts have no safe research multiplier, so the calculator refuses to invent one.

    3 · Negotiation scripts

    Fill in your context once — every script below updates live, and each copies to your clipboard with one tap.

    4 · Red-flag checklist

    Scores the deal's contract structure — not its pay, which the tracker benchmarks separately. Check everything the offer actually contains; your selections persist in this browser.

    5 · Copy my deal memo

    A one-page summary for your records: terms, rate-card score, red flags, and your counters. A negotiation aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Benchmarks disagree 2–3x within tiers — verify against current creator rate reports before you quote.
