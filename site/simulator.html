▸ YouTube Shorts always on

$0.02–$0.20 per 1K views in most niches (creator-reported). The Oct 1, 2026 originality update: recommendations deprioritize re-uploaded/aggregator content, originals get the reach. Re-uploading others' clips won't build here anymore.

Creator Rewards: $0.40–$1.00 per 1K qualified views (US top-tier, creator-reported). Only 1-minute-plus original videos qualify; qualifying views run ~half of raw views. Gate: 10K followers + 100K views/30d, under it, TikTok pays $0 but still builds audience.

Direct payout: $0 per view in 2026 (Reels Play Bonus ended 2023, nothing comparable replaced it). Reels money comes from sponsors, gifts, and subscriptions. Reels past 3 minutes get diminished non-follower distribution, keep them under 3:00 for reach.
