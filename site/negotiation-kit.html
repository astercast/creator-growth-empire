Counter builder Reply scripts Walk-away Brief

1 · Counter-offer builder

The brand's offer, your format, your audience. The counter is the top of your niche-adjusted tier range, with usage, whitelisting, and exclusivity stacked as separate lines, each one carrying the benchmark it came from.

2 · Reply scripts: ready to send

Six situations, six replies, each built from the numbers above. Open the one that matches, edit it in place, your edits hold while you work, and copy it into the thread. Unfilled fields stay visible as {{placeholders}} so nothing ever invents a number. Firm, specific, no pep talk.

3 · Know your walk-away

Your floor is the higher of two numbers: the rate-card floor for this deal's tier, platform, and niche, and your own floor, if you set one in the builder above. The pipeline strip reads the deals you logged in the Deal Room, in this browser only: what is committed, what has actually been paid, and what is still open. No close probabilities are invented.

4 · Copy my negotiation brief

The whole position on one page, the offer, the verdict, the evidence, the counter lines, and your walk-away, for your records or your manager.

A model, not a prediction: benchmarks are negotiation ranges compiled from reported 2026 data, and they disagree 2–3x within tiers. A negotiation aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Contracts above $500 deserve a real lawyer's eyes.
