# Content Repurposing

## Use when
- A long-form pillar video ships (run the SOP every time)
- Planning the weekly content calendar (Phase 3)
- Training an editor on the repurposing workflow

## Inputs
- The finished pillar video (YouTube long-form, 8–15 min)
- Platform list: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, X/Threads
- The atom test score for the idea (standalone hook? visual moment? quotable line? debatable claim?)

## Procedure
1. **Score the atom (1–4).** If the idea scores 1–2 (value only exists in full context), publish the pillar and skip repurposing, save the 2 hours. Only 3–4 scores get the full SOP.
2. **Extract 3 clip candidates.** Timestamp the three best self-contained moments: highest emotion, most surprising fact, strongest opinion. Each must survive without the other 9 minutes.
3. **Short #1 → YouTube Shorts.** Best moment, new 2-second hook, burned-in captions, 9:16 reframe, end card pointing to the full video.
4. **Short #2 → TikTok.** Second moment, native text overlay, trending audio at 10% underneath, 60–90s cut to stay Creator Rewards eligible.
5. **Short #3 → Instagram Reels.** Contrarian clip or hot take, polished caption, 5 hashtags. Most aesthetic version of the three.
6. **Facebook native.** Full pillar uploaded natively to the Page (never a YouTube link) + best short as a Reel. Facebook-native captions, discussion-prompting description.
7. **Text atomization.** The core insight becomes an X/Threads post (hook + 5–7 posts + video link) and a community tab version.
8. **Stagger the schedule.** Don't post everything day one, spread across 5–7 days so each platform's audience gets a clean shot.

## Worked example
Pillar: "I tried 5 AI video tools for 30 days" (12 min, atom score 4).
- Short #1 (Shorts): the side-by-side quality comparison reveal, "tool 3 destroyed the other four."
- Short #2 (TikTok): the funniest AI fail, 75 seconds, trending sound underneath.
- Short #3 (Reels): hot take, "stop paying for [tool], here's the free one that's better."
- Facebook: full 12-min native upload + fail clip as Reel.
- Text: "I tested 5 AI video tools for 30 days. 4 are a waste of money. Here's the one that isn't 🧵"
- Total marginal time: ~2 hours. Total touchpoints: 8+.

## API references
- `GET data/v1/chapters.json` → Phase 3 chapter (platform grammars, the SOP)

## Pitfalls
- Blind cross-posting the identical file, each platform has a native grammar; adapt or get throttled.
- Leaving watermarks on, TikTok watermark on Reels gets downranked. Export clean masters.
- Repurposing weak ideas, the SOP multiplies the idea's quality, good or bad. Score the atom first.
- Posting everything simultaneously, staggering beats dumping.
- Forgetting the funnel, every short should point *somewhere* (the pillar, the channel, the list).
