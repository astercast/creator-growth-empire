Every money tool drops a deal when it is paid. This page picks it up: the board's paid deals, longest since payment first, each with the amount the brand actually paid set against the rate card the same deal builds today, and one copyable book for the whole list.

Deals are entered on the Deal Pipeline, this book reads the same board (same browser, same localStorage). Follow-ups for deals still in play live in the Follow-Through Queue. Pricing belongs to the Rate Card Builder; this page adds no numbers of its own.

1 · The book: paid brands, longest-paid first

Order is longest since the deal entered the paid stage first. That is an ordering, not a renewal-due date, this site carries no renewal-timing data, so the book claims no window for when a brand should be re-approached. Demo paid deals are counted and excluded, never booked as real sponsors. No pitch wording is attached to any item: the repo's pitch templates are a cold pitch, a one-time follow-up nudge, and a warm intro that needs a real mutual connection, none of them describes a pitch to a brand that already paid, so none is mislabeled as one here.

A planning aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice. Amounts are the figures you entered on the board; current pricing is the Rate Card Builder's, built from each deal's own entered figures with its provenance printed.
