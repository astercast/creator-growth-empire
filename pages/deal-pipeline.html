DEAL PIPELINE Your live sponsorship deals on one board — priced by the Rate Card Builder, scripted by the Negotiation Script Builder, totaled from the deals you actually enter.

Six stages — lead, negotiating, terms set, live, invoiced, paid. Every deal carries its rate-card numbers with their provenance, its current negotiation script one click away, and a follow-up nudge when it has sat in a stage too long.

Pricing a card before a deal exists? The Rate Card Builder. Working one thread's words? The Negotiation Script Builder. This page is the board those two tools feed. The board's follow-ups, assembled into one send list: the Follow-Through Queue. The board's paid deals, held as the re-approach book: the Repeat Sponsor Book. The board's paid deals as a month-by-month ledger: the Sponsor Income Ledger. The board's invoiced deals as the register of money owed: the Receivables Register. Every brand on the board as one relationship total — collected, owed, and still open per brand: the Brand Ledger. Every platform on the board as one total across every stage — collected, owed, and still open per platform: the Platform Ledger.
