Durable foundations, volatile terms

The stages above are built to last: niche math, packaging, cadence, community, and deal craft change slowly, so they are taught once, properly. Platform terms — thresholds, splits, program rules — change constantly, and they are not baked into the stages. They are tracked separately in Algorithm Watch, the standing "stay current" layer that runs on top of this path. When a stage lesson points at a live threshold, it sends you to a checker that reads the current terms — check the Watch before you act on any number.
