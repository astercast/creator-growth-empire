How this page works

Work from the top. Stage 1 comes first. Each stage makes the next one make sense.

A step is a read or a do. A read teaches you one thing. A do asks you to make one thing. Each step says what you need, what to do, and what done looks like.

Tick a step's box only when it is done, not when you start it. Your ticks are saved in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere. Leave, come back, and the path remembers where you are.

What changes, and what does not

The steps on this page are built to last: how to pick a topic, make videos, post on set days, grow, and get paid. Those change slowly.

App rules, pay rates, and sign-up lines change all the time. They are not frozen into these steps. They live on the Algorithm Watch page, which tracks what changed. Before you trust any number, check the Watch.
