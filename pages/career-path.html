How this manual names things

Every lesson and tool in this manual is named by one formula, in every place it appears: number, name, kind word, state word. A lesson's number is its stage and its position in the stage (1.1 is the first lesson of Stage 1). A tool's number is its position in the tools listing. The kind word for a lesson is read or drill; for a tool it is its job: Planning, Money, or Content.

The state vocabulary is closed. A lesson or tool is current, or it has been replaced and is marked superseded, with its replacement named. No other state words exist. Difficulty uses five grades only: Very easy, Easy, Moderate, Difficult, Very difficult.

Every lesson opens with its spec block (stage, kind, time it takes, difficulty, state) before any prose, lists what you need, works in numbered steps, and closes with a conclusion line naming the artifact the lesson produces. That artifact is the lesson's definition of done. When you finish a lesson, its stamp prints in its contents line, in residence, a mark in the record. Finish a stage and the stage stamp lands the same way.

The five stages

Check a lesson off when it is done, a read is finished, a drill's criterion is met, not when it is started. Your progress is held in this browser only and never sent anywhere. A stage is complete when every lesson in it is checked; the path always names your next step above.

Where each lesson comes from, labelled on every lesson: Creator Academy: original drill means the drill was authored for this path; Read · from … names the existing chapter or intel file the read is drawn from. No lesson is left unlabelled, and a lesson whose source is not recorded says so instead of guessing.

Durable foundations, volatile terms

The stages above are built to last: niche math, packaging, cadence, community, and deal craft change slowly, so they are taught once, properly. Platform terms, thresholds, splits, program rules, change constantly, and they are not baked into the stages. They are tracked separately in Algorithm Watch, the standing "stay current" layer that runs on top of this path. When a stage lesson points at a live threshold, it sends you to a checker that reads the current terms, check the Watch before you act on any number.
