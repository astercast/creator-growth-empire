The board knows which deals have sat too long. This page is the morning walk, done for you: urgent first, then most overdue — each follow-up with the exact words to send, and one copyable brief for the whole queue.

Deals are entered on the Deal Pipeline — this queue reads the same board (same browser, same localStorage). Pricing belongs to the Rate Card Builder; the words belong to the Negotiation Script Builder. This page adds no numbers and no wording of its own.
